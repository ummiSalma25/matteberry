Can I use a mockup to sell my merch?

October 19, 2026 · By matteberry

Short answer: yes. If you are building a merch brand, the whole point of a matteberry mockup is to help you sell it. Put your design on the JPG or the PSD, then use the finished images to promote and sell your merch.

Where you can use the finished images

  • Your website or online store, on product pages and banners.
  • Instagram, in posts, stories and reels.
  • TikTok.
  • X and any other social page you run for your brand.

Why a mockup helps you sell

People buy what they can picture themselves wearing. A design sitting on a flat file is hard to imagine. On a model, in good light, it looks like something they could order today. That is what a mockup gives you before you have spent anything on a photoshoot.

How to get started

  • Pick a mockup that fits your design and your brand.
  • Open the PSD and drop your design into the smart object. Our step by step guide shows you how.
  • Export the finished image and post it.

Not sure about a specific use?

If you have a question about how you plan to use a mockup, email us at matteberryhq@gmail.com and ask before you post. We are happy to help.
