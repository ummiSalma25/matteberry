Five ways to post one mockup on Instagram and TikTok

October 25, 2026 · By matteberry

One mockup does not have to be one post. With a little planning, a single image can fill a week of content for your brand. Here are five ways to do it.

1. The clean product post

Start simple. Post one clear image where the design is easy to read, with a short caption that tells people what it is and where to buy.

2. Front and back carousel

Many of our collections come with front and back views. Post both as a carousel and end the caption with a nudge to swipe to see the back.

3. Same design, different colors

Put your design on two or three tee colors and ask your followers which one they would pick. It is a quick way to start a conversation, and a story poll works well for this too.

4. File to finished product

Start a story or reel with your flat design file, then reveal it on the model. People like seeing an idea turn into something they could wear.

5. A quick slideshow

Pick three or four mockups and cut them together into a short slideshow for TikTok or Reels. Couple mockups are great here if you sell matching pieces.
